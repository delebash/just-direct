# What's new

## v0.0.1

- **Your films**: make a film with a title, a story and the words for its look, in a wide, tall or
  square shape.
- **Cards**: the characters, places, props and looks a film reuses in every shot — described in
  words, with a picture the AI paints on your computer or you take from a file.
- **Shots**: the AI plans the film from its story — the cards it needs, then every shot — and after
  you change a shot by hand it carries your change into the later shots. Add, change, move and delete
  shots by hand too.
- **First frames**: each shot's first frame painted with its cards' pictures, several at a time,
  checked by a vision model and the best one used; compare them and choose by hand.
- **Clips**: each shot's first frame made into a clip with its sound — one pass, a long take up to
  13 seconds, drawn to the character's voice, led by paths you draw on the frame or by a guide video.
- **Voices**: each shot's line spoken in its character's voice, made from the words on the
  character's card or cloned from a recording you give it (a file, or recorded in the app); a clip
  made after it moves the mouth with it.
- **Cut**: the film's shots on a timeline with their trims and transitions (cut, dissolve, fade), music, songs with your own lyrics and sound effects made from words and laid over the film, and **Export the
  film** as one MP4 to watch and save.
- **More than one window**: the app open in several places at once shows each one's changes in the
  others by itself (**Settings → General → Other windows**).
